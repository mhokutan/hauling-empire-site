Hauling Empire: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026
Who we are: Hauling Empire is developed and operated by Hazım Okutan, an individual developer based in New Jersey, USA ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used (we are the "controller" under the GDPR and the "veri sorumlusu" under Türkiye's KVKK).
Contact: hazimokutan@hotmail.com

This policy explains what data the Hauling Empire app for iPhone, iPad and Android collects, why, how long we keep it, and what choices and rights you have. A Turkish version is also available.

1. Summary

  • You can start playing without signing up. The game creates an anonymous account (a random ID) to back up your progress.
  • You can protect that account with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google on iPhone and iPad, and with Sign in with Google on Android, so you can restore it on a new device. Accounts already linked to Game Center or email keep working.
  • Solo mode is single-player. Only if you agree to a one-time notice in the game, your company name, @username and weekly profit are shown on your region's public Solo weekly board (see §2 and §3); you can leave it at any time in Settings. Online mode is one shared world with other players: your username, company name, colour, emblem and bio, your trucks on the map, leaderboard scores, guild activity and the messages you send are visible to other players (see §3).
  • We collect gameplay analytics and crash reports to fix bugs and balance the game. You can turn analytics off (see §8).
  • In Solo mode, the game offers optional rewarded video ads from Unity LevelPlay on iPhone, iPad and Android: a rewarded ad plays only when you tap a "Watch an ad" button, in return for a small in-game reward. There are no banners. On Android, in Solo and Online, a short video ad may also play at a natural break (after a delivery report closes): never before your first delivery, never over a decision or a purchase, and at most a few times an hour. Players with Hauling Ad-Free, Hauling Pro or an earlier Online licence see no ads and get the rewards without a video. See §5.
  • Our ad partner Unity uses device data, and the advertising ID where you allow it, to deliver ads. Before any ad is loaded, the game asks you once whether ads may be personalized; if you say no, Unity shows non-personalized ads. On iPhone and iPad, Unity gets the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you choose personalized ads and then allow tracking in the iOS prompt. The reward is the same either way, and you can change your choice any time in Settings. We do not sell your personal data for money, and we do not use your account or game data for advertising.
  • You can delete your account inside the game at any time.

3. What other players can see (Online mode and the Solo weekly board)

Your @username, company name, colour, emblem and bio; your trucks moving on the shared in-game map; your leaderboard ranks and scores; your guild membership; whether you are online (you can turn this off); a small PRO mark next to your company, shown only if you have Hauling Pro and turn "Show Pro badge on my company" on (off by default); loads you post or take on the shared board; your guild chat messages (visible to guild members) and your direct messages (visible to the recipient).

Solo weekly board (only if you agreed to the one-time notice): your company name, @username, weekly profit, tier, fleet size and rank on your region's weekly board, and your week on your friends' friends board. If you leave the board, nothing new is shown; a row already there stays until that week leaves the board, at most two weeks.

Do not put personal information (real name, phone number, address, social media accounts) in your username, company name, bio or messages. Offensive words are filtered automatically, and you can block or report any player.

If you use Game Center, Apple shows your Game Center name, scores and achievements to other Game Center players according to your Game Center settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to that.

4. How we use data

Only to: provide the game and its online features; back up and restore your progress; verify purchases; moderate the community and act on reports; detect cheating and abuse; fix crashes and bugs; and understand, in aggregate, how the game is played so we can improve it.

We do not use your account or game data for advertising (the ad partner's own collection is described in §5), we do not sell it, and we do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. Automatic anti-cheat and rate-limit checks may temporarily limit some in-game actions; you can ask us for a human review of any action taken on your account.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase, Inc.: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Servers in the United States (AWS us-east-1, Virginia).
  • Apple Inc.: Sign in with Apple, Game Center, App Store payments and purchase verification (App Store Server API and App Store Server Notifications). Apple also processes this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google LLC: Google Sign-In (your Google account identifier and email address, see §2), Google Play Billing on Android (payments, and purchase verification through the Google Play Developer API using the purchase token; refund and renewal notices from Google Play) and the Google Play install referrer (§2, Install source). Google also processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads; on iPhone, iPad and Android): optional rewarded video ads in Solo and, where switched on, short video ads at natural breaks in Solo and Online. Before any ad is loaded, the app asks you once, on an in-game card, whether ads may be personalized; nothing is sent to Unity before you answer. After that, the Unity software in the app contacts Unity's ad servers when it starts and when an ad is loaded or shown. According to Unity's disclosures it collects, directly from your device, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and operating-system details (model, version, language, screen), app information, device identifiers (the Android advertising ID and app-set ID; on iPhone and iPad the IDFV and, only with your tracking permission, the IDFA), and how you interact with ads (shown, watched to the end, tapped), to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. If you choose Personalized ads, we tell Unity that you consent to personalized advertising (GDPR) and that you do not opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (US state laws), and Unity may use this data to show ads based on your interests. If you choose General ads only, we tell Unity that you do not consent and that you opt out of sale and sharing, and Unity shows non-personalized ads (it still uses the data above to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud). In both cases we tell Unity that the app is not directed to children. The reward is the same either way, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings or Legal. We send Unity no account, profile or game data; the game itself decides the reward. Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings (Privacy > Ads). On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Unity only if you choose Personalized ads and then tap "Allow" in the iOS tracking prompt that appears right after; without that permission Unity gets no IDFA and shows non-personalized ads. You can change the permission any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Content delivery services the app contacts directly. To draw the map, your device downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org, map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). The app's fonts are bundled inside the app, so no font service is contacted. Like any website, this service receives your device's IP address and standard request information (and which map area is being shown). We send it no account or game data. Its own privacy policy applies.

Other players see the information described in §3. We may disclose information when the law requires it, to answer valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of players or the service.

6. International transfers

We and our servers are in the United States. If you play from the EU / EEA, the UK or Türkiye, your data is transferred to the US. For EU / UK data we rely on the safeguards our providers offer (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses). For Türkiye, transfers abroad are made under Article 9 of the KVKK (Law No. 6698), using the safeguards available under that article (such as standard contracts) or, where the law requires it, your explicit consent.

When you delete your account, your Online leaderboard history is kept as "Deleted player", purchase records are kept without your player ID, the free-trial and invite claims are kept only as one-way hashes to prevent abuse, and your gameplay statistics are kept only in anonymous form. Anonymous means that we keep no list, key or other data that links these statistics back to you or to your new account, so they are no longer personal data. Apart from that we keep only an anonymous counter with no identifier: the day of the deletion (not the time) and whether it was a guest account. It does not record the time or how many gameplay events the account had, so it cannot be matched to the anonymous statistics.

8. Your choices

  • Turn off analytics: Me › Settings › Account & cloud save → turn off "Usage data (analytics)". Events not yet sent are deleted from your device. Crash reports are still sent so we can keep the game stable; they contain no contact details.
  • Hide your online status in your online profile.
  • Block or report any player from their profile or from a message.
  • Delete your account: Me › Settings › Account & cloud save › Delete account. Your account and the data linked to it are deleted from our database straight away (§7 lists the few exceptions; your gameplay statistics stay only in anonymous form, with no link to you). If you no longer have the app, you can ask us to delete your account on the web: https://mhokutan.github.io/hauling-empire-site/delete-account.html. Data stored on your device is not touched; start a new game to reset it. Deleting the app does not delete your account. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google: cancel them in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the Google Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Permissions (notifications, photos, camera) can be changed in iOS or Android Settings at any time. The photo picker gives us only the picture you choose, and the camera is used only when you take a feedback picture yourself.

9. Your rights

Write to hazimokutan@hotmail.com and include your player ID (shown in Settings) so we can find your data. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours. We reply within the time the law sets (GDPR: one month; KVKK: 30 days; CCPA: 45 days). Using your rights is free.

EU / EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): access, correction, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest (including analytics), and withdrawal of consent at any time. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Türkiye (KVKK, Article 11): to learn whether your data is processed and to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it is transferred to; to ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected, and for data to be deleted or destroyed, and for these actions to be notified to those third parties; to object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

California (CCPA / CPRA) and other US states: the right to know and access, delete and correct your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. In the last 12 months we collected the categories in §2 (identifiers, commercial information (purchases), internet or electronic activity (gameplay and diagnostics) and user content) for the purposes in §4, and disclosed them only to the service providers in §5 (in Solo mode, the ad network collects the device information described in §5 when ads are used). We do not sell personal information for money. If you choose "Personalized ads", letting Unity use your device data for interest-based ads may count as "sharing" (cross-context behavioural advertising) under California law; choosing "General ads only" (on the first card or any time in Settings) is your opt-out of sale and sharing, and the ad network is then told so and do not use sensitive personal information in a way that requires a right to limit. An authorised agent can make a request for you with your written permission.

10. Children

Hauling Empire is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age for digital consent in your country, which is up to 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game online or given us data, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and stored with our provider, which encrypts data at rest. Database access rules let each player read and change only their own private data; feedback pictures are stored in a private bucket that only the developer's admin account can read; server keys are never included in the app. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

We will update this policy when the game or our practices change and update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game before it takes effect.
