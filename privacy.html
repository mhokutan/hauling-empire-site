Hauling Empire — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026
Who we are: Hauling Empire is developed and operated by Hazım Okutan, an individual developer based in New Jersey, USA ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used (we are the "controller" under the GDPR and the "veri sorumlusu" under Türkiye's KVKK).
Contact: hazimokutan@hotmail.com

This policy explains what data the Hauling Empire app for iPhone and iPad (and any other version we release) collects, why, how long we keep it, and what choices and rights you have. A Turkish version is also available.

1. Summary

  • You can start playing without signing up. The game creates an anonymous account (a random ID) to back up your progress.
  • You can protect that account with Sign in with Apple and link Game Center, so you can restore it on a new device.
  • Solo mode is single-player. Online mode is one shared world with other players: your username, company name, colour, emblem and bio, your trucks on the map, leaderboard scores, guild activity and the messages you send are visible to other players (see §3).
  • We collect gameplay analytics and crash reports to fix bugs and balance the game. You can turn analytics off (see §8).
  • We do not show third-party ads, do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not sell or share your personal data.
  • You can delete your account inside the game at any time.

3. What other players can see (Online mode)

Your @username, company name, colour, emblem and bio; your trucks moving on the shared in-game map; your leaderboard ranks and scores; your guild membership; whether you are online (you can turn this off); loads you post or take on the shared board; your guild chat messages (visible to guild members) and your direct messages (visible to the recipient).

Do not put personal information (real name, phone number, address, social media accounts) in your username, company name, bio or messages. Offensive words are filtered automatically, and you can block or report any player.

If you use Game Center, Apple shows your Game Center name, scores and achievements to other Game Center players according to your Game Center settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to that.

4. How we use data

Only to: provide the game and its online features; back up and restore your progress; verify purchases; moderate the community and act on reports; detect cheating and abuse; fix crashes and bugs; and understand, in aggregate, how the game is played so we can improve it.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. Automatic anti-cheat and rate-limit checks may temporarily limit some in-game actions; you can ask us for a human review of any action taken on your account.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase, Inc. — database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Servers in the United States (AWS us-east-1, Virginia).
  • Apple Inc. — Sign in with Apple, Game Center, App Store payments and purchase verification (App Store Server API and App Store Server Notifications). Apple also processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Content delivery services the app contacts directly. To draw the map, your device downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org, map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). The app's fonts are bundled inside the app, so no font service is contacted. Like any website, this service receives your device's IP address and standard request information (and which map area is being shown). We send it no account or game data. Its own privacy policy applies.

Other players see the information described in §3. We may disclose information when the law requires it, to answer valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of players or the service.

6. International transfers

We and our servers are in the United States. If you play from the EU / EEA, the UK or Türkiye, your data is transferred to the US. For EU / UK data we rely on the safeguards our providers offer (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses). For Türkiye, transfers abroad are made under Article 9 of the KVKK (Law No. 6698), using the safeguards available under that article (such as standard contracts) or, where the law requires it, your explicit consent.

8. Your choices

  • Turn off analytics: Me › Settings › Account & cloud save → turn off "Usage data (analytics)". Events not yet sent are deleted from your device. Crash reports are still sent so we can keep the game stable; they contain no contact details.
  • Hide your online status in your online profile.
  • Block or report any player from their profile or from a message.
  • Delete your account: Me › Settings › Account & cloud save › Delete account. Your account and the data linked to it are deleted from our database straight away (§7 lists the few exceptions). Data stored on your device is not touched; start a new game to reset it. Deleting the app does not delete your account. Subscriptions are billed by Apple — cancel them in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • iOS permissions (notifications, photos) can be changed in iOS Settings at any time. The photo picker gives us only the picture you choose.

9. Your rights

Write to hazimokutan@hotmail.com and include your player ID (shown in Settings) so we can find your data. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours. We reply within the time the law sets (GDPR: one month; KVKK: 30 days; CCPA: 45 days). Using your rights is free.

EU / EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): access, correction, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest (including analytics), and withdrawal of consent at any time. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Türkiye (KVKK, Article 11): to learn whether your data is processed and to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it is transferred to; to ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected, and for data to be deleted or destroyed, and for these actions to be notified to those third parties; to object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

California (CCPA / CPRA) and other US states: the right to know and access, delete and correct your personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. In the last 12 months we collected the categories in §2 — identifiers, commercial information (purchases), internet or electronic activity (gameplay and diagnostics) and user content — for the purposes in §4, and disclosed them only to the service providers in §5. We do not sell or "share" personal information (for cross-context behavioural advertising) and do not use sensitive personal information in a way that requires a right to limit. An authorised agent can make a request for you with your written permission.

10. Children

Hauling Empire is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age for digital consent in your country, which is up to 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game online or given us data, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and stored with our provider, which encrypts data at rest. Database access rules let each player read and change only their own private data; feedback pictures are stored in a private bucket that only the developer's admin account can read; server keys are never included in the app. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

We will update this policy when the game or our practices change and update the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game before it takes effect.
