Hauling Empire

Start as a single driver hauling loads across a real-world road map, buy your own trucks, hire drivers and grow a logistics company. Play Solo at your own pace, or join Online mode — one shared world with other players, a shared load board, guilds and weekly leaderboards. For iPhone and iPad.
