Hauling Empire: Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 24, 2026
App: Hauling Empire for iPhone, iPad and Android, developed and operated by Hazım Okutan, an individual developer based in New Jersey, USA ("we", "us")
Contact: hazimokutan@hotmail.com

This page explains how to delete your Hauling Empire account and the data linked to it, what is deleted, what we keep and for how long, and how to ask us to do it if you no longer have the app. A Turkish version is also available, and a Spanish one (versión en español) at https://mhokutan.github.io/hauling-empire-site/delete-account-es.html Our Privacy Policy is at https://mhokutan.github.io/hauling-empire-site/privacy.html

1. Delete your account in the app

This is the fastest way. The deletion happens straight away and you do not need to write to us.

Where you playPath in the appGood to know
iPhone and iPadMe › Settings › Account & cloud save › Delete accountIf you protected your account with Sign in with Apple, Apple shows its confirmation sheet once so we can end the app's access to your Apple ID. A subscription is cancelled in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
AndroidMe › Settings › Account & cloud save › Delete accountA subscription is cancelled in the Google Play Store app › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  1. Open the game and tap Me.
  2. Open Settings and scroll to Account & cloud save.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the message, and tap Delete account again in the confirmation window.
  4. The game shows "Your account and cloud data were deleted."

You do not have to sign up first: the button is also there for the anonymous (guest) account the game creates for you. If you changed phones or reinstalled the game and you protected your account with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, install the game, sign in with the same account and delete it from there.

Deleting the app does not delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Deletion is permanent: we cannot bring your company or progress back afterwards. The game files on your device are not touched; start a new game in Settings to reset them.

2. Ask us to delete it if you no longer have the app

If you cannot open the game any more, ask us by email:

  1. Write to hazimokutan@hotmail.com with the subject "Delete my Hauling Empire account".
  2. Tell us your @username or company name and whether you play on iPhone, iPad or Android. If you have it, add your player ID (shown in Me › Settings › Account & cloud save) and, if you protected your account, the email address of your Apple or Google account.
  3. To protect other players from false requests we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours (for example by writing from the email address of the Apple or Google account linked to it, or by giving the player ID or a purchase receipt). If we cannot confirm it, we may decline and tell you why.
  4. We answer within 30 days. Once the account is confirmed as yours we delete it as described below and tell you when it is done.

Asking is free. Your rights under the GDPR, the KVKK and California law (CCPA / CPRA) are described in section 9 of the Privacy Policy.

3. What is deleted and what stays

Deleted straight away from our database (in the app at once, or when we process your email request): your account and sign-in, your cloud save and company, your Online profile (@username, company, colour, emblem, bio), friends and blocks, the direct messages and guild messages you sent, your guild membership, the feedback you sent with its picture, your crash reports, your Solo weekly board rows, and your device key. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also end the app's access to your Apple ID.

What stays after deletion (copied from section 7 of the Privacy Policy):

Gameplay statistics (events and daily play time)Kept only without any link to you: your account number is replaced with a random one and names and free text are removed. Only the day of each event is kept, not the time. We keep no list or key that links them back to youKept for game statistics

Apart from that we keep only an anonymous counter with no identifier: the day of the deletion (not the time) and whether it was a guest account. It does not record the time or how many gameplay events the account had, so it cannot be matched to the anonymous statistics.

Apple, Google and Unity (the ad partner) process some data under their own privacy policies, and we cannot delete what they hold. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

4. Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel them. Cancel in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in the Google Play Store app › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.
